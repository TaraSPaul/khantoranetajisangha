Welcome to Our Legacy

A PROUD HERITAGE CLUB

A proud heritage club of Howrah in the heartbeat of Domjur Khantora, Khantora Netaji Sangha is where tradition, culture, and community spirit thrive together. Recognized as one of the most culturally vibrant clubs in the region, we uphold Netaji’s ideals through service, sports, and social commitment.

Our campus is a hub of activity and excellence:

Sports & Fitness

Thrilling football games on our home ground, disciplined Taekwondo training for youth, and a state-of-the-art Multi-Gym for all-round fitness.

Arts & Recreation

Graceful Dance classes, a dedicated Card & Carrom room for camaraderie, plus a peaceful Guest Lounge & Library for members.

Festivals & Events

Hosts of Khantora’s grand Durga Puja and the iconic Gold Cup Football Carnival that unites the community.

Social Service

Regular Blood Donation camps on birth anniversary of Netaji and year-round initiatives for the upliftment of society.

More than a club, we are a family and a movement. At Khantora Netaji Sangha, heritage inspires action, culture builds character, and service defines us.
